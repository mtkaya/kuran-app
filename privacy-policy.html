Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

📋 Introduction

Kuran Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

🔒 Key Point: Kuran Pro does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. Your privacy is our priority.

📱 Data Collection

Kuran Pro does not collect any personal information. All app data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third parties.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Reading progress and last read position
  • Bookmarked verses
  • Personal notes you create
  • App preferences and settings

🔊 Audio Streaming

When you listen to Quran recitations, audio files are streamed from public Quran audio servers. No user data or personal information is transmitted during this process.

🚫 Third-Party Services

Kuran Pro does not use any:

  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Third-party data processors

👶 Children's Privacy

Kuran Pro is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

🔐 Data Security

Since all your data remains on your device, you have full control over it. We recommend keeping your device secure and backing up your data regularly.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated revision date.
